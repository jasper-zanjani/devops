There are two pricing models available for Azure SQL Database.

-   DTU (Database Transaction Unit) is a blended measure that combines compute, storage, and IO which abstracts the cost of database performance.<br/>Service tiers:
    -   Basic
    -   Standard
    -   Premium


-   The **vCore-based pricing model** where compute, storage, and IO resources are independently scaled.

    -   General Purpose
    -   Hyperscale
    -   Business Critical
    -   The free database offer includes 100,000 vCore seconds, 32 GB of data, and 32 GB of backup storage free per month.

An additional choice of compute tier also affects price:

    -   Provisioned
    -   Serverless
