List the two largest cities in Mexico (by population)

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

```sql title="SQL"
SELECT City 
FROM north_american_cities 
WHERE Country == "Mexico" 
ORDER BY Population Desc 
LIMIT 2;
```

```kusto title="Kusto"
North_american_cities 
| where Country == "Mexico" 
| sort by Population desc 
| take 2 
| project City
```
